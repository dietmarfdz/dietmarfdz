## About me

I am a **Computational Biologist and Senior Bioinformatics Scientist** with 15+ years of experience in genomics, transcriptomics, multi-omics analysis, and large-scale biological data integration across research, clinical, and translational environments.

My work spans the full genomic data lifecycle: from NGS workflows and sequencing data processing to quality control, harmonization, statistical analysis, biological interpretation, and the development of scalable and reproducible bioinformatics pipelines.

I have worked with **WGS, WES, RNA-seq, GWAS, population genomics, biomarker discovery, and large biomedical datasets**, combining biological knowledge with scientific computing and high-performance computing.

My current interests focus on **computational biology, multi-omics integration, precision medicine, machine learning and AI-ready biomedical data**, particularly in applications aimed at understanding disease mechanisms and supporting therapeutic research.

## Featured project

### [CBIcall WGS + mtDNA Slurm Orchestrator](https://github.com/dietmarfdz/cbicall-slurm-wgs-mtdna-orchestrator)

A Slurm orchestration and monitoring layer for sequential **CBIcall WGS and mitochondrial DNA analyses**.

The project adds:

- Slurm dependency orchestration;
- WGS → mtDNA workflow chaining;
- per-stage validation;
- mtDNA failure diagnostics;
- low-mtDNA-signal classification;
- batch execution and monitoring;
- persistent per-sample status logging;
- conservative fail-safe cleanup of large WGS BAM intermediates.
